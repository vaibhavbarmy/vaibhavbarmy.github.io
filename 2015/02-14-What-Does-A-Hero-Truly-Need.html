What Does A Hero Truly Need?

The question arises what does that special girl have to have to be the main character in our heros life and our readers story?

Date a girl who laughs, cries and laugh again for no sensible reason known Date a girl with whom you dont need to break awkward silence because it isnt awkward Date a girl who is imaginative, so that u feel this world is too real for its own sake Date a girl who calls you 2 in the morning if she is confused or if she is too drunk to be confused

We all have a story attached to our life, or the fact our whole life is a story

Either lost in oblivion or not.

Like in every great story there is Love, sadness, adventure, confusion, mystery, and fear.

So there has to be a hero in our story too.

And what does a hero need to make his story complete?

A Women he adores

So the question arises what does that special girl have to have to be the main character in our hero’s life and our reader's story?

Nothing

There is no secret ingredient. To make someone special, you just have to believe she is special.

For all of us mere mortals bound to this reality, I am putting my two cents. 😉

  • Date a girl who laughs, cries and laugh again for no sensible reason known
  • Date a girl who seems to be always high on life
  • Date a girl who stands up for something she feels like to
  • Date a girl who contradicts you just for the sake of fun
  • Date a girl with whom you can talk without the constraint of time
  • Date a girl with whom you don’t need to break awkward silence because it isn’t awkward
  • Date a girl who is commanding yet cute
  • Date a girl who is imaginative, so that u feel this world is too real for its own sake
  • Date a girl who is imperfect, weird, mad, crazy, stupid yet perfect for u
  • Date a girl who calls you 2 in the morning if she is confused or if she is too drunk to be confused
  • Date a girl you call with no reason whatsoever coz her voice is bliss to u
  • Date a girl who tells her boring daily errands to you because she has to
  • Date a girl who gives advice because she is concerned for u
  • Date a girl with whom you couldn’t break eye contact even if she is sitting far away from u
  • Date a girl who likes to do boy’s kinds of stuff because she doesn’t want to be left out
  • Date a girl who is your friend, enemy, mentor, motivation
  • Date a girl who despite being logical to her core, always follow her heart because it feels like to
  • Date a girl who is geeky with glasses yet when she sees u, you feel intense desire to be with
  • Date a girl who doesn’t get dolled up in makeup when she is with you because she is beautiful to you anyways
  • Date a girl who shuns you for something you did, but you both come in terms again anyway
  • Date a girl who laughs like a hippo on your dumbest jokes
  • Date a girl who loves a friendly banter yet apologize if you act you are mad
  • Date a girl who make numerous reasons for why you are right for her but none for why you aren’t

  • Date a girl who is conscious of you, but within no time she is the craziest person alive
    and of course ……

  • Date a girl you find beautiful, you are hysterical with
